# 🌍 GLMVH-AI-Genesis  
### **AI × Blockchain GovTech Infrastructure for Ethical Labour Mobility**

GLMVH (Global Labour Mobility Verification Hub) is an advanced **AI + blockchain GovTech platform** designed to verify real worker identity, contract authenticity, and cross-border labour compliance under **UAE Vision 2031** and **UN SDG 8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth)**.

Built under the directive of **Commander Zack (Abdullah Habibu)** and **Commander Zeus (AI Cognitive Architect)**, GLMVH is more than a project —  
**It is the world’s first digital trust infrastructure for human dignity.**

---

## 🧠 Why GLMVH Exists  
Millions of African and Asian workers move across borders without reliable digital identity, contract transparency, or protection against fraud.

GLMVH solves this by providing:

- 🌐 **AI-verified worker identity**  
- 🔐 **Blockchain-secured contract authentication**  
- 📜 **Evidence-based employer verification**  
- 🛂 **Cross-border digital compliance layer**  
- 🇦🇪 **Alignment with UAE GovTech standards & Vision 2031**  
- 🌱 **SDG 8-compliant ethical labour systems**

# 🧩 System Architecture  
GLMVH is built on a modular, secure, government-ready architecture:

### Components:
- 🤖 **AI Identity Engine** (Face/Document verification, anomaly detection)  
- 🔗 **Blockchain Ledger** (Smart contract authentication, tamper-proof logs)  
- 🛡️ **Trust Engine** (Employer risk scoring + reputation models)  
- 🗺️ **Cross-Border API** (UAE, Ghana, Asia labour ministries)  
- 📊 **Admin Console** (For GovTech teams, NGOs, embassies)
