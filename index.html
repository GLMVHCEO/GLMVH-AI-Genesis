Why GLMVH Exists

Millions of African and Asian workers cross borders without reliable digital identity, transparent contracts, or shared trust between recruiters, employers, and ministries. GLMVH turns fragmented paperwork into a single, verifiable truth layer.

Worker-first protection

  • AI-verified worker identity linked to official documents.
  • Machine-checked contracts with fraud and anomaly alerts.
  • Portable reputation that travels with the worker, not the agency.

State-grade transparency

  • Blockchain-secured logs for every contract, change, and consent.
  • Ministry dashboards to monitor recruitment agencies in real time.
  • Evidence-based employer verification and corridor risk scoring.

Aligned with UAE Vision 2031

  • Positions the UAE as the world’s first ethical labour mobility hub.
  • Transforms labour corridors into trusted digital infrastructure.
  • Demonstrates AI for public good, not only productivity.
Outcome: fewer disputes, faster onboarding, safer workers. Value: confidence for ministries, recruiters, and global employers.

System Architecture

GLMVH AI-Genesis is modular, API-first, and deployment-ready for sandbox pilots with ministries, embassies, and verified recruitment partners.

Core trust components

AI Identity Engine

OCR + document checks, anomaly detection, and optional biometric signals to create a verified worker and employer profile.

Blockchain Ledger

Smart-contract authentication and tamper-proof logs for contracts, approvals, and dispute outcomes.

Trust Engine

Reputation models for agencies and employers, corridor risk scoring, and compliance alerts for ministries.

Cross-Border API

Secure interfaces for UAE, Ghana and Asian labour ministries, embassies, and authorised GovTech partners.

Admin Console

Dashboards for government teams, NGOs, and oversight bodies to monitor live worker flows and intervene when needed.

Who GLMVH Serves

  • Ministries & regulators — real-time oversight, corridor analytics, and provable enforcement of ethical recruitment rules.
  • Recruitment agencies — single portal to verify candidates, publish compliant contracts, and build a trusted brand.
